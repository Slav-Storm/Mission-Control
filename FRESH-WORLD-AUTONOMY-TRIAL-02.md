# Fresh-World Autonomy Trial 02

5 October 2026 — **LEVEL 2 — EARLY AUTONOMY**. Trial stopped safely and preserved.

Fresh commissioning, the bounded Director opening strategy, and the epoch-zero viewer now work in real Minecraft. The colony independently completed eight small surveys through the ministry request system. It then reported a genuine resource-acquisition/crafting boundary. This is not sustained industrial autonomy, a five-day pass, or approval for the final filmed run.

## What changed after Trial 01

Trial 01 remains failed evidence, unchanged. Its three startup gaps were addressed in the existing architecture:

1. **Native commissioning:** a declared empty run receives authenticated bootstrap observations, enrolls a gateway and independently observed turtles, assigns new persistent logical identities, binds runtime and current recipe generation, collects readiness acknowledgements, commits its first epoch, and verifies operational acknowledgements. It does not import a legacy controller, fabricate a prior handover, or inherit physical capability availability.
2. **Opening strategy:** `BEGIN_MONIFACTORY_PROGRESSION` creates a bounded Director objective. The Director examines verified capabilities, inventory, knowledge and fuel; Production planning exposes prerequisites; supported work passes through existing Exploration and Fleet requests. Completion events cause replanning. Unknown or unsupported paths produce structured blockers rather than model calls or invented execution.
3. **Native viewer/history:** an empty epoch-zero run is valid. Initialization with an unknown Minecraft day is recorded separately from the first physical day sample. Observed bootstrap entities appear during commissioning; the same run transitions to epoch one and live operation without importing history or resetting the map.

The opening planner is deliberately limited: three candidate infrastructure needs, bounded recipe expansion, one unfinished opening child, and at most eight surveys before reassessment requires engineering. These limits prevent an unlimited search loop; they also limit the autonomy demonstrated here.

## Independent acceptance before this trial

The three changes were tested before Trial 02 began. A separate disposable physical fixture commissioned eleven members, established epoch one without legacy state, and completed two Director-selected survey jobs. Its state was not imported into Trial 02.

Current-source regression passed **30 PC suites, including 412 Node tests and the required Lua-dependent suites**, plus **22 legacy/viewer suites, including 30 viewer tests and the legacy Lua tests**. Isolation, fresh fencing, unsupported planning, epoch-zero projection and existing migration/recovery behaviour were covered. Simulated regressions and real Minecraft proofs remain distinct evidence sets. Earlier fixture failures were retained.

The Director found no verified shared storage, crafting capability or furnace. Planning exposed chest, crafting-table and furnace prerequisites; no usable wood source was known. It selected bounded nearby surveys as the supported next action rather than pretending those production processes were available.

The request chain was:

`Director opening objective → Exploration SURVEY_AREA → Fleet execution/allocation → turtle observation → verified receipt → Director replan`

Eight distinct turtles performed eight verified survey jobs. The bounded executor records these physical observation jobs under its existing `INSPECT_INVENTORY` operation, while their parent requests and completion evidence are surveys. Mapping grew from 30 to 56 cells. Native observations distinguish solid blocks from `emptyOrFluid`; an unsuccessful block inspection is not labelled proven air.

Exploration and Fleet performed physical work. Production contributed planning blockers. Logistics recorded observed turtle inventories. Mining and Construction had no legitimate productive prerequisites and did not manufacture work to appear busy. The ten turtles remained intact. Peak simultaneous active jobs was **one**; this trial does not demonstrate concurrent fresh-start progression.
