# Fresh-World Autonomy Trial 03

7 October 2026. **LEVEL 2 — EARLY AUTONOMY. Engineering and trial results are partial; first infrastructure has not been demonstrated.** The accepted PC migration remains accepted. Trial 03 does not establish sustained Monifactory progression or readiness for the final filmed run.

## What actually happened

A new disposable Survival world and unique run were initialized with the exact previous allowance: one advanced computer, ten advanced turtles, eleven wireless modems, ten diamond pickaxes and 640 coal. No chest, crafting table, furnace, hammer, wood, saplings, food, ores or extra fuel were supplied. All ten turtles independently equipped their permitted tools/upgrades and consumed 64 coal through native mechanics, reporting 5,120 fuel each. Initial cargo was empty. Equipment placement was observed, the one-shot bootstrap was sealed outside the world, and no terrain-clearing or progression assistance was performed. An initial invocation before reload finished returned Unknown function with no placement effects; the later successful invocation was verified and never repeated.

Before commissioning, world-specific ledger tables were empty. Portable knowledge did not create physical capabilities. Eleven observed members established fresh epoch 1; no legacy authority or old map was imported. The player remained stationary. Development and Trials 01/02 were preserved separately.

The Director received broad opening intent and chose survey work from missing infrastructure ingredients. Codex did not choose individual turtle jobs or resource coordinates. There were three separately identified operating attempts, with source changes only between safely held attempts. This is an engineering-assisted trial, not one uninterrupted autonomous success.

## Timing and stopping

The target was up to five Minecraft days. It was **not reached**. Native telemetry records integer day samples, not enough intra-day timing to claim exact fractional operational days. The three host operating-and-drain intervals total approximately 1973.2 seconds; that is not Minecraft-day duration. Commissioning, pauses and engineering time are excluded from autonomous claims.

Attempt 1 stopped at its configured 24-survey ceiling. Attempt 2 added 30 surveys before the existing 32-direct-child ledger guard blocked its flat request tree. Both results remain preserved. Independent tests of a batching repair then exposed the separate 128-request-per-root guard. The repaired Director forecasts survey, execution and Fleet descendants before admission. It keeps both generic limits unchanged and reports a planning-budget blocker instead of dispatching work whose dependency tree cannot fit. It does not silently create replacement roots.

**A search/request budget is a software ceiling, not proof that the world contains no useful resources or that no frontier remains.** The final structured blocker is `STRATEGIC_BLOCKER:OPENING_PLANNING_BUDGET_EXHAUSTED`. Continued strategic search beyond this finite lifecycle is a remaining implementation limitation.

## Early crafting: the real boundary

Installed static mechanics and a real turtle probe agree: a pickaxe-plus-modem turtle has **no `turtle.craft` API** without a workbench upgrade. The installed first crafting-table recipe requires two flint and two logs; knowing that recipe does not make a turtle a player with a 2×2 crafting grid.

The first isolated probe incorrectly called the absent API and failed; its evidence remains. The corrected probe reports `CRAFT_API_UNAVAILABLE`. No free crafting API, player crafting, extra bootstrap workbench or spawned ingredients were used. First legitimate crafting infrastructure therefore remains **unavailable**, not completed. A legitimately acquired workbench and a supported acquisition/commissioning path, or an explicitly human-approved rule decision, would be needed; neither is assumed. No challenge-rule change was made.
