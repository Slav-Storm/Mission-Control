# Concurrent operations checkpoint — 17 September 2026

The existing development colony was preserved. No clean world was created. A
local checkpoint of all ComputerCraft application state and software preceded the
work. No terrain files, seed information, player input or spawned resources were
used for the demonstrated physical chain.

## Physical results

- Mission Control generated twelve physical objectives across finite supervised
  windows: three surveys, four useful ore deliveries, one grove harvest, one
  planting repair and three refuels. Codex configured policy, rather than
  assigning each productive robot individually.
- Survey, forestry and ore delivery overlapped on three robots. The independent
  local witness captured 35 such samples; the compressed historical archive
  retained 22 concurrent-operation samples. These are samples, not a claim of
  uninterrupted three-robot activity throughout the test.
- Cassiterite-sand cargo was consolidated into an existing chest up to its target
  of 64. Reported stored vanadium magnetite increased to 19. Storage ownership
  prevented competing access; no quest stock was moved.
- Mapping grew from 9,291 to 9,774 observed cells. Repeated surveying at a location
  stopped when the last bounded survey produced no new knowledge.
- Grove care recovered 29 new spruce logs. Three beds were planted initially;
  snow obstructed the fourth. A separate repair physically verified all four
  saplings and left eight spare saplings. The failed attempt remains recorded.
- Once the robot returned, the manager detected fuel demand, reserved carried
  timber, and generated three refuel objectives. Each consumed four logs and
  gained 60 fuel. Robot fuel increased from 989 to 1,169; twelve physical logs
  were consumed. The measured yield is 15 fuel per log in this installation.
- Instrumented fleet totals at verification were 1,070 fuel consumed and 180
  replenished. WATCH is correct: this first grove recovery and its long access
  route were not fuel-positive. Net-positive renewable operation remains a
  development target, not an achieved result.

## Preservation and regression

All ten original identities returned fresh idle telemetry. The checkpoint's
244 jobs, 39 commands and all 9,291 map cells remain represented. Detailed retired
records are host-archived before controller compaction. The 911-quest catalog,
eleven bounded recipes and source hashes remain intact.

Iron Supply retains 24 reserved iron ingots. Movin' Around retains its reserved
wooden hook. Both remain physically staged according to the existing storage
evidence; neither was submitted, claimed or consumed. Fresh readiness expires
to STAGED as designed, without releasing reservations.

All sixteen Python/Lua test programs and nineteen JavaScript tests passed.
Coverage includes shared ownership, priorities, fuel accounting, duplicate/old
telemetry, command lifecycle, run isolation, storage, recursive crafting, furnace
production, selective inventory, quest orchestration, retention and replay.
Existing production paths were regression-tested without consuming staged stock.

The live observer showed simultaneous activity. During the repair/refuel chain,
the browser displayed an earlier day while current telemetry kept changing.
Replay advanced from day 26 to 27; LIVE returned to current authoritative state.
The browser remains read-only. Local history and the daily report exporter remain
operational; detailed telemetry is not published.

## Failures retained and corrected

An initial controller serialization alias stopped the controller before work was
dispatched. A later retention transition exposed a missing-field assumption in a
compact forestry receipt after all robots had returned. Both were repaired and
recovered through the existing adjacent-computer service mechanism, with no world
reload, job replay or erased records. Regression tests cover both defects.

One completed delivery was initially marked FAILED because its inventory receipt
preceded a longer return journey. Exclusive, job-attributed evidence now survives
that journey. A separate reconciliation verified the delivery without moving the
items twice; its original failure remains. Snow-blocked planting was also kept as
a failure, reconciled as partial work, and repaired by a new physical job.

## Deliberate stopping point

The bounded job allowance is exhausted. Automatic work generation is stopped;
the controller, live telemetry, viewer, timeline and reporting remain running.
Two robots retain explicit recovery-reserve assignments; other idle reasons are
visible. Further operating windows can use the same validated policy interface.

The manager has bounded work generators, not universal autonomous industry.
Carried fuel is supported; courier refuelling, distant rescue, new machinery and
more efficient fuel production remain future work. No unlimited unattended
harvest was enabled during this first acceptance test.
